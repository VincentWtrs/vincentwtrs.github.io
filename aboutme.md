I am a data scientist / AI expert based in Belgium. I design and build data-driven solutions across various domains in the financial sector, including investments and financial markets, marketing, real estate, and insurance. From 2018 to 2024, I worked at [KBC Bank & Insurance](https://www.kbc.be/retail/en.html) (Belgium) as a data scientist. Since 2024, I have been working as a data scientist at [Bank Van Breda](https://www.bankvanbreda.be/) (Belgium).

My work spans all stages of the data science project lifecycle, from scoping and analysis to implementation, delivery, and maintenance. A selection of projects includes:

* AI-driven investment fund ([Enhanced Intelligence Fund](https://newsroom.kbc.com/a-first-for-belgian-kbccbc-clients-kbc-asset-management-introduces-a-new-way-of-fund-management)): the first Belgian AI-driven investment fund. For historical performance data, see [Morningstar](https://global.morningstar.com/en-eu/investments/funds/0P00000JKW/quote).
* Generative AI-driven document handling system for Know Your Customer (KYC) processes, automatically extracting and converting information from legal documents.
* Use of Large Language Models (LLMs) and other generative AI techniques to automate and assess claims handling for non-life insurance.
* Customer Relationship Management (CRM) applications, e.g. lead generation (propensity-to-buy) prediction using machine learning classifiers.
* [Real Estate Valuation Tool](https://newsroom.kbc.com/another-brick-in-the-wall-the-belgian-urge-to-build-their-own-home): a data-driven valuation tool for land, apartments, and houses across Belgium.
* [Bolero Matti](https://newsroom.kbc.com/smart-investment-assistant-matti-puts-together-tracker-portfolio-tailored-to-the-clients-needs): a robo-advisor for exchange-traded funds (ETFs).
* [KBC Kate Chatbot](https://newsroom.kbc.com/kate-your-personal-digital-assistant): a personal assistant integrated into the KBC mobile application using natural language processing.

In the past, I also taught an introductory course, "Machine Learning with Python", at the [FLAMES](https://www.flames-statistics.com/) Institute for Statistics, organized by the Flemish universities during their annual [Summer School](https://www.flames-statistics.com/summerschool) in 2021, 2022, and 2023.
